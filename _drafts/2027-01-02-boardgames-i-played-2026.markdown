#### [![Slay the Spire]({{ site.url }}/assets/boardgames-2026/slayTheSpire.jpg){: style="clear:right;margin-left:20px;float:right;width:125px;"}](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D8LZ6W9L/)[Slay the Spire](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D8LZ6W9L/)

Slay the Spire, the board game, is impressive how much it maps to the original video game. It's a pretty cool feeling actually holding the cards in your hand that you're used to seeing on the screen. It's a fun game, but personally, I don't think it's a great board game. There's a lot of toil in updating the health, shield, cooperatively figuring out a strategy, and most of all, the statuses to the characters and monsters. It felt like a board game that had all the expansions built in. We played for several hours, and got through half of one Act. It was fun, but I think it needs a tighter, slimmed down version that's easier to pick up and play with a satisfying payoff.
