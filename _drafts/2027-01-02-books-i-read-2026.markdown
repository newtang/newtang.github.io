This is the third book of the Stormlight Archive and it is looooong. It's also very heavy on lore, and I frequently had to look up who or what they're talking about. The last 15% or so is very compelling, but it really felt like a slog to get there. I'm probably going to take a break from Stormlight.

#### [![Heart and Steel]({{ site.url }}/assets/books-2026/heartAndSteel.jpg){: style="clear:right;margin-left:20px;float:right;width:125px"}](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1982175796/)[Heart and Steel](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1982175796/) by Bill Cowher

I was pleasantly surprised by how much I enjoyed Bill Cowher's autobiography. As with most autobiographies, there's likely some light revisionist history, or some uncomfortable details left out. But, generally I found his perspective genuine, and it was fun to relive some Steelers memories from the 90s and early 2000s. I think I enjoyed his perspectives on leadership and management even more than the insights from Steeler seasons. A must read for a Steelers fan!
